Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

S3VEN Social Manager is a personal social media management application used to organize and schedule multilingual video content through Postiz.

Information We Process

When authorized by users, the application may process account identifiers, usernames, profile details, authentication tokens, video content, captions and publishing settings.

How We Use Information

We use this information to connect authorized accounts, organize content and manage scheduled publications.

Third-Party Services

The application integrates with social media platforms such as TikTok using their official APIs.

Storage and Security

Application data is stored through a self-hosted Postiz installation with appropriate access controls.

Data Sharing

Information is shared with connected platforms as necessary to provide authorized functionality. Personal data is not sold to advertisers.

Data Deletion

Users may disconnect authorized accounts and request deletion of their stored data by contacting the application operator.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion requests, contact the application operator using the contact information provided with the application.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when application functionality changes.
